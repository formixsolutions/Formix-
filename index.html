01. On-page SEO Content

Helpful content, natural keyword targeting, title tags and meta descriptions, heading structure, internal links, image alt text, and content optimization.

02. Off-page SEO Authority

Ethical backlink opportunities, relevant digital PR, brand mentions, guest-content planning, and social distribution. No guaranteed rankings or spam-link promises.

03. Technical SEO Performance

Indexing and crawlability, mobile usability, Core Web Vitals, page speed, XML sitemaps, HTTPS checks, canonical signals, redirects, and broken links.

04. Local SEO Local reach

Google Business Profile guidance, local keyword research, consistent business details, location pages, and a responsible review-response process.

05. E-commerce SEO Products

Product and category content, duplicate-page handling, structured data checks, internal navigation, and shopping-feed recommendations where relevant.

06. SEO reporting Measure

Prioritized action plans and agreed reporting using available analytics and search performance data. Some checks require access or specialist tools.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, deadlines, and required support. Message FORMIX on WhatsApp or email for a tailored quote.

Can you work with a business in another country?

FORMIX offers remote collaboration for clients in different markets. Availability, time zones, payment arrangements, and project scope should be confirmed directly.

Does the website health form automatically scan my site?

No. This is a static HTML website, so the form prepares an email request only. A genuine automated scanner would need a scanning service or backend and appropriate security controls.

Can you guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No responsible SEO provider can guarantee a specific ranking. SEO improvements depend on many factors, including competition, site condition, content, and search engine changes.
